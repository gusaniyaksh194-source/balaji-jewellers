About Us

Serving Jamnagar Since 2014

Balaji Jewellers is a trusted jewellery store located in Gandhi Market, Shop No. 3, Chandi Bazar, Jamnagar. Since 2014, we have been providing quality jewellery with honest service and customer satisfaction.
